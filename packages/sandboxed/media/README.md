# Registry artwork

`icon.png`: 256 × 256 Forms sibling of the approved EmQuix SEO family icon.
`banner.webp`: 1536 × 512 featured image for the QuixForms registry page.
Both are declared under `release.artifacts` in the source manifest. The official
CLI measures, hashes and uploads these images as release media.

`sandbox-builder.webp` and `submissions-inbox.webp` are real screenshots from the
disposable, registry-installed Core site. The Contact us demonstration form and
three submissions were created through the actual UI/public renderer. Names and
example.com addresses are synthetic. One entry was marked read and starred.
Lossless WebP compression preserves the captured controls; no simulated UI or
visitor data is used. These images appear in `release.artifacts.screenshots`.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool from the approved Forms icon;
optimized for delivery without adding frontend dependencies.

Banner prompt: "Create one polished very wide 3:1 landscape banner, ideally
1536 by 512 pixels. Match the EmQuix website: warm ivory background #f5f3ed,
crisp near-black typography, restrained burnt orange accents. On the left,
generous negative space and exact large title 'QuixForms' with Quix near-black
and Forms burnt orange; below in smaller dark clean sans serif the exact tagline
'Powerful Forms. Effortless Building.'; above the title a small uppercase eyebrow
'FREE FORMS FOR EMDASH'. On the right place the supplied approved Forms icon,
preserving its orange geometric emblem, charcoal rounded-square tile and Forms
label, like a flat brand asset rather than a 3D object. Subtle thin orange
geometric rule or outline detail only, spacious professional composition.
Preserve strong contrast and readable text at small widths. No UI screenshots,
no invented dashboards, no people, no mockup frames, no extra words, no other
logos or watermarks. Output the banner alone filling the wide canvas."
